# Nomenclature

a.b.c.d.name.R

a: asymptotic dependence (0) or independence (1)

b: are parameters of the EVDs estimated (0), or is there marginal transformations (1)?

c: conducted on original scale (0) or log scale (1)?

d: bias correction, no (0) or in probability space with symmetric maximal (1) or asymmetric maximal (2)? *Symmetric maximal means the bias term is taken as an absolute max over angles and for the tube above and below. Asymmetric maximal means a different bias term for above and below, but maximal over all angles.*
